THE LOCATION FINDER
A minimal site to know your current location geo coordinates (longitude and latitude degrees) in a real time, by just clicking the show location button and instantly the coordinates will be shown.
Then you can view you location on the real map by clicking the coordinates generated below, to view your location on the real map.
